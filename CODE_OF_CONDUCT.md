# Code of Conduct

AMSB follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive in technical discussion.
- Treat benchmark results as evidence about configurations, not about
  people or vendors.
- Do not harass, discriminate, or make personal attacks.
- Respect the no-fabrication rule: unsupported capabilities are reported
  honestly.

Maintainers enforce this code of conduct. Reports of unacceptable behavior
go to the maintainers directly.
